# LinkedIn: 30-Day Posting Plan

## Brief

- Prepare 30 LinkedIn posts for one month.
- Make every post distinct, using different approaches and formats.
- Keep all 30 posts together in this master file.
- Create a separate folder for each day (`Day 01` through `Day 30`).
- Day 01 is prepared as a Halloween trick-or-treat candy-hunt experience for October 31.

## Day 01 — Halloween: Trick-or-Treat Candy Hunt

**Format:** Interactive Halloween page with three clickable haunted houses, animated candy reveals, a candy-bag counter, optional spooky sound, and a congratulatory champion screen after all three doors are opened.  
**Engagement prompt:** Invite readers to open all three doors, count their candy, and challenge a friend to beat their score.

**Post copy (English):**

> **TRICK OR TREAT? KNOCK IF YOU DARE!** 🎃
>
> Three spooky houses. A bag full of surprises. 👻  
> Click each front door to see what treats are hiding inside — and count how many you can collect!
>
> Which house will you knock on first? Try them all, then challenge a friend to beat your candy score. 🍬
>
> Play the interactive Halloween candy hunt: https://halloween-candy-hunt.vercel.app
>
> #Halloween #TrickOrTreat #CandyHunt #HalloweenFun

**বাংলা সংস্করণ:**

> **ট্রিক অর ট্রিট? সাহস থাকলে দরজায় টোকা দাও!** 🎃
>
> তিনটি ভুতুড়ে বাড়ি। আর ভেতরে লুকানো মজার মজার ক্যান্ডি! 👻  
> প্রতিটি দরজায় ক্লিক করে দেখো ভেতরে কী ট্রিট লুকানো আছে—আর গুনে দেখো, কয়টা ক্যান্ডি জিততে পারো!
>
> প্রথমে কোন বাড়ির দরজায় টোকা দেবে? সবগুলো চেষ্টা করো, তারপর বন্ধুকে চ্যালেঞ্জ দাও—কে বেশি ক্যান্ডি জিততে পারে! 🍬
>
> ইন্টারঅ্যাকটিভ হ্যালোইন ক্যান্ডি হান্ট খেলুন: https://halloween-candy-hunt.vercel.app
>
> #হ্যালোইন #ট্রিকঅরট্রিট #ক্যান্ডিহান্ট #হ্যালোইনমজা

**Assets:** See the `Day 01` folder for the interactive candy-hunt page and bilingual post copy. Opening all three doors reveals a trophy, champion badge, animated confetti, the final candy score, and a replay button. LinkedIn does not run interactive HTML inside a regular post; publish the page to a web host, then share its link (or post a screen recording).


## Day 02 — "Do You Love Me?" Interactive Love Button

**Format:** Interactive pure HTML/CSS/JS page with cute bear and panda characters, a dodging "No" button, a growing "Yes" button, floating hearts, and a confetti celebration.  
**Engagement prompt:** Invite readers to try catching the "No" button, then send it to someone they love.

**Post copy (English):**

> **Do you love me?** 💖
>
> A tiny question with a very stubborn "No" button. 😏  
> Every attempt makes "Yes" bigger, and when you finally say Yes, hearts and confetti are everywhere. 🎉
>
> Built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Play it: https://love-button-animation.vercel.app
>
> #HTML #CSS #JavaScript #WebDevelopment #FrontEnd #CodingProjects #ValentinesDay

**Assets:** See the `Day 02` folder for `do_you_love_me.html` and bilingual `post_copy.txt`. Live at https://love-button-animation.vercel.app
