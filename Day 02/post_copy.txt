DAY 02 — "DO YOU LOVE ME?" INTERACTIVE LOVE BUTTON

Do you love me? 💖

A tiny question with a very stubborn "No" button. 😏
Try to click it... if you can! Every attempt makes "Yes" bigger, and when you finally say Yes — hearts and confetti everywhere. 🎉

Built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Zero libraries.

Play it here: https://love-button-animation.vercel.app

Try it, then send it to someone you love. 💕
