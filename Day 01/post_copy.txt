ALT TEXT

A dark, playful Halloween candy hunt with three spooky illustrated houses. Visitors click each house to open its door, see a ghost or bat peek out, and add animated candy to a candy-bag counter. Optional spooky sounds can be enabled.

ASSET NOTES

- Interactive candy hunt: open halloween_trick_or_treat.html in a browser, then click each house to reveal candy.
- Sound is optional and starts only after the visitor turns it on.
- To share the interactive experience on LinkedIn, publish the HTML page and add its public link to the post.
